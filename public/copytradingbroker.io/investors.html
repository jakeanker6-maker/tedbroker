Investors

Why invest through TED Brokers

Market access

Trade US-listed stocks and ETFs, US-listed options, and 30 cryptocurrencies quoted in USD from one account.

Order tools

Market, limit, stop, stop-limit and trailing stop orders, dollar-amount orders, and extended-hours limit orders for stocks and ETFs.

Portfolio and reporting

An equity chart and allocation view, monthly statements and trade confirmations in PDF, and a realized gains report in CSV.

Platform overview

Key features to review before you open an account:

01

Account types

Cash accounts and margin accounts. Margin requirements and margin calls apply. Short selling is available for stocks and ETFs in margin accounts, not for crypto.

02

Options

US-listed options on stocks and ETFs, including 11 multi-leg strategies filled as one order. Each contract covers 100 shares. Options approval levels apply.

03

Markets

US-listed stocks and ETFs (more than 10,000 symbols), options, and 30 cryptocurrencies quoted in USD. Crypto trades around the clock.

04

Fees

Stocks and ETFs: $0 commission, with regulatory pass-through fees on sales. Options: $0.65 per contract. Crypto: 0.50% per trade.

Order controls and reporting

Stop, stop-limit and trailing stop orders define exit points on your positions. Margin requirements apply in margin accounts. If you use copy trading, which is optional, you set a copy stop-loss for each copy.

Stop orders

Stop, stop-limit and trailing stop orders help you define exit points on your own positions.

Portfolio view

An equity chart and an allocation view by holding, so you can review how your portfolio is weighted.

Live charts and alerts

Live charts, watchlists, a stock and ETF screener, and price and percent-move alerts. Delayed quotes are clearly labelled.

TED Brokers

Professional trading across US stocks, ETFs, options and crypto, with market data, advanced order tools and portfolio reporting.

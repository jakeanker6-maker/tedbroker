FAQs

TED Brokers is an online trading platform for US-listed stocks, ETFs, options and cryptocurrencies. It offers live charts, watchlists, a stock and ETF screener, market, limit, stop, stop-limit and trailing stop orders, cash and margin accounts, a portfolio view and monthly statements.

Copy trading is an optional feature. You can choose a strategy profile, allocate cash, and have your account mirror that profile's trades in proportion to your allocation.

To open an account, register with your email address and complete your profile with your name, contact number and country of residence. Before you can fund your wallet or trade, we verify your identity. This requires a valid government-issued photo ID, such as a passport or driving licence, and a proof of address dated within the last three months, such as a utility bill or bank statement.

If we need a clearer document or another file, we will email you and state what is missing. Options trading is subject to an options approval level.

You can fund your wallet by bank transfer or cryptocurrency. Request the deposit in your wallet. The balance is credited once the deposit has been approved.

To withdraw funds, request a withdrawal in your wallet. Withdrawals need a confirmation code and approval before they are processed.

Copy trading is optional. Copied trades carry market risk. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results, and market conditions can change quickly. You could lose some or all of the cash you allocate to a copy.

Use a copy stop-loss to set a drawdown limit for each copy, and pause or stop copying at any time. Stocks, ETFs, options and cryptocurrencies all carry risk, so only allocate money you can afford to lose.

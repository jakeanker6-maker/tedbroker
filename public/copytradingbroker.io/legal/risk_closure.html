Risk Closure

Trading stocks, ETFs, options and cryptocurrencies, like any financial activity, carries risks that every client should understand before trading. This page outlines the main types of risk involved in using the TED Brokers platform and the measures we take to manage them. Optional copy trading carries the same market risks as the strategy profile it follows.



1. Market Risk

Market risk is the possibility of losing capital because prices move against your position. Stock, ETF, option and cryptocurrency prices can change quickly, and options can lose value rapidly as expiry approaches. Margin amplifies both gains and losses. Global economic events, interest rate changes and investor sentiment can all significantly affect prices. If you use optional copy trading, your copied positions carry the same market risk as the strategy profile you follow.


2. Credit Risk

Credit risk arises when a counterparty or service provider, such as a bank or payment provider, fails to meet its financial obligations. Funding by bank transfer or cryptocurrency depends on third-party services. We review our service providers and monitor transactions for compliance. Credit risk cannot be entirely eliminated.


3. Liquidity Risk

Liquidity risk is the risk of being unable to execute an order quickly and at the expected price because there are few buyers or sellers. It is most pronounced in thinly traded stocks and options and in cryptocurrencies during volatile periods. Limit orders may remain unfilled, and extended-hours trading, where available, can have lower liquidity. Review the liquidity of an instrument before placing an order.


4. Operational Risk

Operational risk encompasses potential losses resulting from failures in internal systems, technology glitches, human error, or external disruptions such as cyberattacks. These risks can disrupt trade execution or result in data breaches. At TED Brokers, we employ advanced security protocols, real-time monitoring systems, disaster recovery plans, and continuous staff training to maintain system integrity and operational continuity. Despite best efforts, some unforeseen risks may still occur.


5. Legal and Regulatory Risk

The financial trading and investment environment is subject to evolving laws and regulations. Regulatory changes in different jurisdictions may impact how the platform operates or how users are allowed to participate. These risks include, but are not limited to, restrictions on trading, tax implications, or the classification of financial instruments. We actively monitor regulatory developments and work closely with compliance specialists to ensure that our services remain lawful and transparent. Users are also encouraged to consult legal or tax professionals based on their personal circumstances.


6. Risk Management Strategies

We provide tools that support risk management, including market, limit, stop, stop-limit and trailing stop orders, price and percent-move alerts, and portfolio statements and reports. For optional copy trading, you set a copy stop-loss and an allocation for each copy, and you can pause or stop copying at any time. These tools can reduce risk but do not eliminate it. You must evaluate your own risk tolerance and make independent decisions based on your objectives.


7. Conclusion

Trading through our platform offers unique opportunities, but it also involves inherent risks that should not be overlooked. Understanding the nature and scope of these risks is essential for any responsible trader. At TED Brokers, we are committed to maintaining a secure and transparent trading environment. We provide the tools, resources, and support necessary to help users navigate these complexities with confidence. It is the responsibility of each user to stay informed, monitor their investments, and use the platform in a way that aligns with their financial objectives and risk appetite.

01 — Trading platform

The tools active traders use, in one workspace.

Place orders, analyse markets, manage margin and review your portfolio without leaving the Trading Hub.

How the platform works
  1. 01

    Order types for every plan

    Market, limit, stop, stop-limit and trailing stop orders, dollar-amount orders for fractional shares, and extended-hours limit orders. Each order is previewed with its estimated cost and fees before you confirm.

  2. 02

    Options, including multi-leg strategies

    Trade calls and puts on US stocks and ETFs. Place spreads, straddles, strangles, iron condors, butterflies, calendars, covered calls and collars as one order, with access set by your options approval level.

  3. 03

    Cash or margin accounts

    Choose a cash account or a margin account. Margin accounts support short selling of stocks and ETFs, with initial and maintenance requirements shown before you trade.

  4. 04

    Research, alerts and screening

    Live charts, watchlists, a stock and ETF screener, company profiles with news and earnings dates, and price or percentage-move alerts.

  5. 05

    Reporting and automated investing

    Monthly statements, trade confirmations and realized gains reports with FIFO or average-cost basis. Invest in ETF model portfolios or set recurring weekly, biweekly or monthly purchases.

02 — Copy trading (optional)

Follow a strategy when you prefer.

Allocate part of your account to a strategy profile. Its trades are mirrored in proportion to your allocation, within the limits you set. Copy trading runs alongside your own trading and can be paused or stopped at any time.

Risk controls

Risk managed, not ignored.

Limits and checks run before and after every order, so exposure stays visible and within your account's requirements.

Order checks Before you trade
Limit and stop prices must sit within 50% of the last price. Every order shows its estimated cost, fees and effect on buying power.
Margin requirements Automatic
Initial and maintenance requirements apply to margin positions. Margin calls are shown with a due date, and withdrawals are restricted while a call is open.
Copy stop-loss Per copy
Choose the drawdown at which copying stops. Copied positions are closed at market price, and the cash is returned to your account.
Options approval Tiered
Strategies are unlocked by approval level, so higher-risk structures such as naked options require a higher level.

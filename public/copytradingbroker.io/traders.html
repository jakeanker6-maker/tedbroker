How it works

Four steps to copy a strategy.

Copy trading uses the same account, funds and order system as your own trading.

  1. 01

    Choose a strategy profile

    Review the strategy, the markets it trades and how it invests before you allocate.

  2. 02

    Allocate cash

    Move part of your available cash into a dedicated copy allocation. It stays reserved for copying until you change or stop the copy.

  3. 03

    Trades are mirrored

    Each trade is copied in proportion to your allocation. Copy purchases are paid from your allocation.

  4. 04

    Monitor, adjust or stop

    Follow the copied positions in your portfolio. Add or withdraw funds, pause, or stop copying. Stopping closes the copied positions at market price and returns the cash.

Your controls

You decide how much to copy and when to stop.

Every copy has its own limits, and you can review them at any time from your account.

  • Allocation: you set it

    Choose how much cash to allocate, from your available balance.

  • Copy stop-loss: automatic

    Set the drawdown at which copying stops. The copy stops automatically, and its positions are closed at market price.

  • Pause and resume: any time

    Pause mirroring while you review, then resume when you are ready.

  • Stop copying: any time

    Close the copied positions at market price and return the cash to your available balance.

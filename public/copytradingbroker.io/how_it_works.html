Order workflow

From idea to confirmed order.

The order ticket checks each order before it is placed, so you see the cost, fees and effect on your buying power first.

  1. 01

    Choose the instrument

    Search by symbol, add it to a watchlist, or find it with the screener. Company profiles, news and charts are one click away.

  2. 02

    Build the order

    Select buy or sell, the order type and time in force. Enter a quantity in shares or a dollar amount. Choose extended hours for limit orders on stocks and ETFs.

  3. 03

    Review the preview

    The preview shows the estimated cost or proceeds, fees, buying power after the order and the resulting position. Nothing is sent until you confirm.

  4. 04

    Confirm and track

    Open orders update live. Fills, cancellations and expirations appear in your order history, and each trade has a confirmation you can download.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Visit the help center
When can I trade?

Stocks and ETFs trade in the regular session, 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. New York time, on US trading days. Limit orders can also be placed in pre-market and after-hours sessions. Cryptocurrencies trade around the clock.

What does a "Delayed" label mean?

It means the price shown is not a live streaming quote. Quotes that are too old are treated as stale, and market orders are never filled on a stale quote.

Can I sell short?

Yes, in a margin account, for stocks and ETFs that are available to borrow. Short positions carry margin requirements and, for some stocks, borrowing costs. Cryptocurrencies cannot be sold short.

When do trades settle?

Stocks, ETFs and options settle one business day after the trade (T+1). Cryptocurrency trades settle immediately. Each confirmation shows the settlement date.

How are fees charged?

Stocks and ETFs have no commission. Sales carry regulatory pass-through fees. Options cost $0.65 per contract, and cryptocurrency trades cost 0.50% of the trade value. Every fee is itemised in the order preview.

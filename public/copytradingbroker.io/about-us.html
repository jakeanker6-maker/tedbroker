About TED Brokers

A professional trading platform for US markets.

TED Brokers offers US-listed stocks, ETFs, options and cryptocurrencies through one account, with live charts, advanced order types, margin and portfolio reporting. Copy trading is available as an optional feature.

01 — Our mission

Give investors a professional-grade platform for trading US stocks, ETFs, options and cryptocurrencies.

Serious trading requires accurate market data, reliable order execution and clear records. We built TED Brokers to provide those tools in a single account.

Charts, watchlists, a screener, limit and stop orders, margin and portfolio statements are designed to work together, so you can plan, place and review your trades in one place.

02 — Our vision

Clear pricing, accurate information and full control over every order and position.

We aim to show fees, order details and account terms before you confirm anything, and to label delayed market data clearly.

Copy trading is an optional feature for investors who prefer to mirror a strategy profile. You choose the allocation, can set a copy stop-loss, and can pause or stop copying at any time.

How it works

One account. Every move of a pro, mirrored.

When a verified trader opens or closes a position, your sub-account mirrors it automatically — in proportion to the amount you allocate, and within the limits you set.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Visit the help center
Can I stop copying anytime?

Yes. You can pause or stop copying a trader from your dashboard at any time. When you stop, you choose whether to close the copied positions at the current market price or keep them open and manage them yourself.

What happens if a trader loses?

Your copied positions lose in proportion to your allocation. A copy stop-loss lets you set the maximum drawdown you'll accept from each trader — if it's reached, copying stops and those positions close.

How much do I need to start?

You can fund your wallet from $50. Some traders set a higher minimum allocation, which is shown on their profile before you copy.

How are fees charged?

Each trader's subscription fee and performance commission are shown on their profile before you start. Subscriptions are charged monthly; performance commission is charged only on profit above your account's previous high.

01 General

What is copy trading?

Copy trading lets you automatically mirror the positions of a trader you choose. When they open or close a trade, the same trade is placed in your account in proportion to the amount you allocate to them.

Who are the traders I can copy?

Traders listed on TED Brokers have a track record on the platform. Each profile shows past returns, maximum drawdown, win rate and a 1–10 risk score, so you can compare them before you copy. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

Which languages is support available in?

The site and our support team work in 10 languages, including English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Arabic and Chinese.

02 Account & verification

What documents do I need to verify my account?

A valid government-issued photo ID (passport, national ID card or driving licence) and a proof of address dated within the last three months, such as a utility bill or bank statement.

How long does verification take?

Most applications are reviewed within one business day. If we need a clearer photo or another document, we'll email you with exactly what's missing.

04 Copy trading & risk

How do I stop copying a trader?

You can stop copying at any time. Open the trader from Copy traders in your dashboard and choose Stop copying. You can also pause a copy if you only want to stop new trades for a while.

What is a copy stop-loss?

A limit you set for each trader you copy. If your copy of that trader falls by the amount you choose, copying stops automatically and the copied positions are closed.

Can I lose money copy trading?

Yes. Copied trades carry the same risk as the trader's own trades, and past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Only invest money you can afford to lose, and use a copy stop-loss to cap your downside.

05 Security

How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?

Go to Settings, then Security, and choose Enable 2FA. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Authy, then enter the 6-digit code to confirm.

What should I do if I think my account is compromised?

Change your password straight away, make sure 2FA is on, and contact support by live chat or phone so we can secure your account and review recent activity with you.

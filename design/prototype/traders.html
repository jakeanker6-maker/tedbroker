How you're paid

Earnings system.

Three income streams, each tied to how well your followers do.

  1. 01

    Subscription fees

    A fixed monthly fee for each follower copying your strategy, shown to followers before they start.

    $2per follower / month

  2. 02

    Performance commissions

    A share of the profit you make for followers, charged only above a high-water mark: if a follower's account falls, you earn nothing from them until it's back above its previous peak.

    15%of profit above high-water mark

  3. 03

    Volume bonuses

    Tiered rebates on the trading volume your followers replicate, paid by TED Brokers. Followers aren't charged extra.

    $3.50per lot copied · tiered

Requirements

Who qualifies.

We only list traders whose record we can verify. You'll need all four of these to apply.

Meeting the thresholds doesn't guarantee a listing — every application is reviewed.

  • 1+ year verified track record

    Twelve months or more of trading history we can verify from your broker statements or a connected account.

  • 3+ consecutive profitable months

    At least three months in a row with net positive returns — consistency, not one lucky month.

  • Max drawdown under 30%

    Your largest peak-to-trough decline stayed below 30% across the verified period.

  • Completed KYC

    Identity and address verification — the same checks every TED Brokers account completes.

01 — How it works

How copy trading works.

You don't need to analyse charts every morning. Connect with proven traders and mirror their moves — with limits you set.

The full walkthrough
  1. 01

    Create & verify your account

    Sign up in minutes, then complete identity verification so your account is ready to fund.

  2. 02

    Fund your wallet — bank, crypto or PayPal

    Deposit by bank transfer, BTC, ETH or USDT, or PayPal. Fees and timings are shown before you confirm.

  3. 03

    Pick a trader by return, risk and style

    Compare track records, maximum drawdown, win rate and a 1–10 risk score before you allocate.

  4. 04

    Copy automatically, stop anytime

    Positions are mirrored in proportion to your allocation. Pause, set a copy stop-loss or stop copying with one click.

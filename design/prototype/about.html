About TED Brokers

We make professional trading accessible to everyone.

TED Brokers connects everyday investors with traders who have a public, verified track record. You see every number before you copy, every trade as it happens, and you stay in control of your risk.

01 — Our mission

Make expert trading strategies accessible to everyday investors through transparent copy trading.

For most people, professional trading has meant either learning it all yourself or handing your money to someone you can't see. Neither is a fair choice.

We built TED Brokers so you can follow traders whose results are public, copy their trades in proportion to what you choose to allocate, and set the limits that protect your balance.

02 — Our vision

A world where anyone can grow wealth by learning from proven traders, with full transparency and control.

We believe the best way to learn the markets is to watch people who do it well, in real time, with real numbers: returns, drawdowns, win rates and risk scores, not marketing.

That's why every copy can be paused or stopped at any moment, and why we show fees and timings before you confirm anything.

03 — Advantages

Why investors choose us

  1. 01

    Transparent track records

    Returns, maximum drawdown, win rate and a 1–10 risk score are published for every trader before you copy.

  2. 02

    Real-time copying

    When a trader opens or closes a position, the same trade is mirrored in your account in proportion to your allocation.

  3. 03

    Built-in risk controls

    Set a copy stop-loss and allocation limits on every trader you follow, and pause or stop copying at any time.

  4. 04

    Segregated client funds*

    Client deposits are held separately from the company's own operating funds.

  5. 05

    Multilingual 24/7 support

    Talk to a person by live chat, email or phone, around the clock and in 10 languages.

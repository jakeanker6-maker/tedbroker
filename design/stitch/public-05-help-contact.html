How do I start copy trading on TED Brokers? add

Navigate to the Copy Trading Terminal, select from our catalog of audited Signal Providers, and define your risk allocation. You can set maximum drawdown ceilings, proportional lot sizing, or direct multiplier modes. Mirroring initiates on the next tick with zero synthetic slippage.

Are trader performance records and historical drawdowns verified? add

Yes. Every transaction record displayed on TED Brokers is pulled directly from live clearing bridge journals. We employ strict cryptographic verification to prevent retroactive metric inflation, cherry-picked backtests, or artificial volume staging.

Can I pause or stop copying a trader at any moment? add

Instant disengagement is accessible with a single click. When unlinking, you may elect to either close open positions at prevailing market price or retain them under manual account management.

How long do withdrawals take? add

Bank transfers settle in 1–3 business days; crypto withdrawals typically within 24 hours after security review. All client funds remain held in segregated Tier-1 custodial accounts.

What is the minimum initial deposit to mirror a master trader? add

Standard account tiers commence at $500 USD equivalent. Specific algorithmic high-frequency signal providers recommend minimum allocations of $2,500 USD to satisfy fractional margin ratios and maintain micro-lot granularity.

What happens if a signal provider experiences a drawdown or loss? add

Drawdowns copy proportionally based on your chosen risk multiplier. You can pre-configure Automated Hard Loss Limits (e.g., auto-detach if drawdown exceeds 12%), protecting your remaining balance unconditionally.

How are performance fees and high-water marks calculated? add

Performance fees operate strictly under the High-Water Mark (HWM) framework. Signal providers only receive success cuts (typically 10%–20%) on net new profits generated beyond prior peak portfolio value.

03 // INSTITUTIONAL KYC & API PROTOCOLS 3 ENTRIES
What documents are required for Tier-1 KYC verification? add

A government-issued photographic ID (Passport or National Identity Card) accompanied by proof of residence issued within 90 days (bank statement, utility invoice, or registered tax documentation). Corporate entities require certificate of incorporation and UBO disclosures.

Can I customize allocation limits and automated stop-loss thresholds? add

Yes. Our terminal supports granular risk parameter configurations per provider: asset exclusions (e.g., disable crypto pairs while mirroring FX), custom slippage caps, and dynamic equity trailing stop triggers.

Are API keys and FIX protocols supported for institutional accounts? add

We provide FIX 4.4 and high-throughput WebSocket REST APIs for algorithmic trading desks, proprietary family offices, and quantitative funds. Cross-connects available directly at Equinix LD4 and NY4 data centers.
